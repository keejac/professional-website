Math 2.0 Skill Builder

MobyMax · Adaptive learning product · 2017–2021

I authored curriculum for MobyMax’s Math 2.0 Skill Builder, contributing to the redesign of the company’s flagship K–8 math product. My work focused on creating clear instructional and assessment items built on strong visuals that aligned to Common Core standards and supported adaptive learning pathways.

What I Built

Math 2.0 Skill Builder delivers targeted practice through thousands of standards‑aligned problems. I authored content across multiple grade levels, designing problem flows, visual scaffolds, and feedback that supported conceptual understanding and procedural fluency.

  • Developed curriculum across grades 2–9, including geometry, measurement, fractions, algebra, and functions
  • Designed problem types that balanced clarity, rigor, and accessibility
  • Created visual scaffolds to support conceptual reasoning and reduce cognitive load
  • Wrote feedback that guided learners through misconceptions and next steps
  • Collaborated with designers and developers to ensure UI/UX consistency across problem templates

Radius, diameter, and circumference of a circle

Curricular design and assessment set
MobyMax

For this strand, I defined the scope and structure of the foundational skills behind radius, diameter, and circumference, the first measurements of circles that students encounter in Grade 7 geometry. I defined the conceptual targets, constraints, and specifications for problem types that introduce each term, clarify their relationships, and build spatial understanding without prematurely invoking formulas or π.

My work included planning the sequence of instruction for the strand, identifying common misconceptions, and designing problem types that give students a firm base in precise vocabulary, visual reasoning, and the connections between these measurements and perimeter concepts from earlier grades. I authored exemplar problem sets that emphasize clarity, accurate spatial interpretation, and readiness for subsequent strands on circumference and area.
